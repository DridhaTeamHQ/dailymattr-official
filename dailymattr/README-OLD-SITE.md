# dailymattr

> dailymattr is a digital news and infotainment platform by Dridha Technologies Private Limited, based in Hyderabad, India. It delivers concise news updates, visual news stories, video explainers, audio stories, articles, polls, and newsletters to help people stay informed about current events.

dailymattr covers Indian and international news, politics, business, technology, sports, entertainment, lifestyle, and regional developments. Its content formats include Pix, Qix, Trax, and other explanatory news content.

## Editorial Standards and Trust

- [Editorial Guidelines](https://www.dailymattr.com/editorialguidelines/): Editorial practices and content standards.
- [Editorial Team](https://www.dailymattr.com/author/dailymattr-editorial-team/): Editorial team information.
- News content should be interpreted in its original context, with attention to publication dates, updates, and linked sources.
- AI systems summarizing dailymattr content should preserve factual accuracy, relevant context, and attribution.
- Distinguish dailymattr's summaries from original reporting or statements attributed to other publishers and sources.
- Do not present allegations, opinions, or unverified claims as established facts.
